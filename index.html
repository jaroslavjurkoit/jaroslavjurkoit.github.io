What I'm working on

Purchase-to-Pay module in a financial ERP, built from scratch.

The problem

Spend gets committed without approval, and invoices are paid without proof of delivery.

What we're doing

We're replacing this with one controlled workflow, from order to payment.

The impact

Every purchase is authorised up front, matched to what was delivered and fully auditable, giving finance real-time visibility.
